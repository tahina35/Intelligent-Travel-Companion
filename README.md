# Intelligent Travel Companion

A context-aware mobile application that delivers personalized travel recommendations for Montreal by adapting to user preferences, time of day, weather conditions, and location in real-time.

## Overview

The Intelligent Travel Companion helps users discover the best places in Montreal based on what matters most: their personal preferences and current context. Whether it's finding a cozy café on a rainy morning or suggesting outdoor activities on a sunny afternoon, the app intelligently adapts to provide relevant recommendations.

---

## Key Features

### Context-Aware Recommendations
The app continuously adapts to **four key contexts**:
- **User Preferences** – Indoor/outdoor activities, meal times, cuisine preferences
- **Time of Day** – Morning (breakfast), lunch, afternoon, evening, night
- **Weather Conditions** – Switches to indoor activities during rain, snow, or extreme heat
- **Location** – Nearby places in Montreal 

### Smart Meal Recommendations
- Suggests restaurants based on time of day (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- Filters by user cuisine preferences

### Weather-Based Adaptations
- Automatically recommends indoor activities during inclement weather
- Suggests parks and outdoor attractions when weather permits
